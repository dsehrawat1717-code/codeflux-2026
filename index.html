🚨 CRITICAL PROTOCOL

What To Do If You Have Already Shared Information or Money

Don't panic! Act immediately within the "Golden Hour" to freeze transactions, lock compromised identity documents, and disconnect remote spy software.

📞 DIAL 1930 (TOLL-FREE) National Cyber Crime Helpline (Golden Hour Freeze)
💳

1. If You Shared Bank OTP, UPI PIN, or Transferred Money

Time Limit: 0-2 Hours
  1. Call 1930 Cyber Fraud Helpline immediately: Report the scammer's UPI ID / bank details. 1930 activates the Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting System (CFCFRMS) to freeze money in the scammer's bank account before they withdraw it.
  2. Call Your Bank Emergency Hotline: Ask them to immediately block your Debit/Credit card, Net Banking access, and UPI VPA.
  3. Note Down Transaction UTR / Ref Number: Copy the 12-digit UTR number from your bank SMS. This is legally required to reverse or freeze the fraudulent transfer.
Submit Complaint on cybercrime.gov.in →
📱

2. If You Clicked a Link or Installed an App (APK / AnyDesk)

Immediate Device Isolation
  1. Turn ON Airplane Mode Immediately: Sever mobile data and Wi-Fi instantly. This stops malware or remote desktop tools (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport) from streaming your screen and OTPs to scammers.
  2. Revoke Device Admin & Accessibility Permissions: Go to Settings → Apps → Special App Access → Device Admin Apps and deactivate any unfamiliar applications.
  3. Uninstall Suspicious App or Factory Reset: Delete the downloaded APK file from your Downloads folder. If your device behaves erratically, back up personal photos and perform a clean Factory Data Reset.
🪪

3. If You Shared Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, or ID Photos

Prevent Identity Theft
  1. Lock Aadhaar Biometrics Online: Open the official UIDAI mAadhaar app or visit myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in. Turn ON "Lock Biometrics" so no one can use your fingerprint or iris for SIM purchases or microloans.
  2. Monitor Credit Bureau Reports (CIBIL / Experian): Check your free annual credit report to ensure scammers have not opened instant loan applications in your name.
  3. File an Identity Theft Incident Report: Lodge an official cyber report documenting that your identity documents were fraudulently obtained.
Lock Aadhaar on myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in →
⚖️

4. If You Were Threatened with "Digital Arrest" or Fake Police

Legal Clarification
  1. FACT: There is NO "Digital Arrest" in Indian Law: The Supreme Court of India, CBI, Police, and ED NEVER arrest citizens or conduct trials over Skype, WhatsApp video calls, or demand money for bail/clearance.
  2. Hang Up and Block Immediately: Do not stay on the video call. Scammers use fake police uniforms and virtual courtroom backgrounds to intimidate victims.
  3. Report Number to Chakshu (Sanchar Saathi): Report the scammer's phone number and WhatsApp handle to DoT to revoke their telecom connection across India.
Report Impersonator on Chakshu Portal →
